The pipeline from [Part 2](/federated-learning/fl-pipeline/). Kubernetes does not know this is federated learning. It sees a Deployment, a Job, a Service, and a PVC.

<LikeC4View viewId="workloads" />

Trainer pods poll Redis until a round is `OPEN`, train on their shard, write weights to the PVC, then increment the round `done` counter. When every client has reported, Redis is `READY_TO_AGGREGATE` and the FedAvg job averages the checkpoints.
